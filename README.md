## Jay

[jay-assistant.fr](https://jay-assistant.fr)

Jay fills in the CRM so the salesperson doesn't have to. Before a meeting, it sends a briefing: deal history, latest interactions, company news, the contact's communication style. During the meeting, it records in person, joins the video call, or simply takes a voice note dictated between two appointments, WhatsApp included. Afterwards, contacts, deals, notes and meetings are created in the client's CRM once they've been approved.

The product isn't mine: I'm its developer, the only one on the technical side, working alongside its founder. Mobile app, web app, back end, CRM integrations and AI pipeline.

- React Native (Expo) mobile app, shipped on iOS and Android
- Native integrations with HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho, Microsoft Dynamics, Odoo, TeamLeader and Notion
- Calendars and messaging: Google Calendar, Microsoft Calendar, Gmail, Outlook, WhatsApp
- LLM-based transcription, extraction of sales actions and meeting summaries
- Supabase back end: Postgres, edge functions, RLS, scheduled jobs
